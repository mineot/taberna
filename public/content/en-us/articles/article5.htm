Casa do Ipê News

News, gatherings, and ideas from a fictional cultural space created for the neighborhood.

July 2025.

The doors are open

After months of sweeping, painting, and listening to suggestions, Casa do Ipê welcomed its first visitors. The old townhouse on Flower Street now holds a reading room, a shared workshop, and a small backyard ready for conversations, performances, and unscheduled afternoons.

This is the first issue of our newsletter. From now on, it will bring together news from the house, open activities, and recommendations from the people who take part in the space each month.

What happened here

More than forty people visited the house during the opening gathering. The book exchange table started with twenty titles and ended with thirty-two. We also received seedlings for the backyard, two lamps for the reading room, and an entire box of mugs that do not match — which suits us perfectly.

Our first workshop was led by illustrator Lia Campos, who proposed an emotional map of the neighborhood. Children and adults drew trees, shops, shortcuts, and important places that do not appear on official maps. The collaborative display will remain on view throughout the month.

Recommendation of the month

Our suggestion is to create a small circulating bookshelf where you live or work. Start with a few books, add a note explaining that they can be taken, and invite others to leave new titles. A shared library does not need to begin on a large scale; it only needs to allow the first book to find another reader.
