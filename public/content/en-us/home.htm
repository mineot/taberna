Taberna

A foundation for your personal website, portfolio, or landing page, with the freedom to adapt the content and design to your needs.

Taberna combines Vue with HTML content and runs entirely in the browser. You can customize the project and publish your site as static files, without a backend.

Explore the project on GitHub

How it works

The application handles the structure and navigation, while the content lives in separate HTML fragments organized by language. This lets you update text and other information without changing Vue components, and maintain content for different audiences.

Learn how it works

Customize your site

Themes

Colors, fonts, spacing, and other visual details can be adjusted to reflect your project's identity. Theme settings help keep the different parts of your site consistent.

Learn more about themes

Layout and composition

Build your site with layout, header, navigation, footer, and sidebar components. The tbc-layout-linear organizes the page, and you fill its regions with the fragments and features that suit your project.

Components and utilities

Navigation, link, carousel, and language components provide reusable features. For styling, the project combines Tailwind CSS with its own utilities for colors, spacing, borders, and other visual details.

Learn more about components Learn more about utilities

Start your project

Fork Taberna on GitHub to maintain your own copy. Then customize the text, branding, images, and design while tracking your changes in the repository.

When you are ready, the project generates the files you can publish to a static hosting service. You keep control of your content, customizations, and choice of hosting provider.
