Taberna

A foundation for creating your personal site, portfolio, or landing page, with the freedom to adapt the content and the visuals to your own style.

Taberna combines Vue with HTML content and runs entirely in the browser. You can customize the project and publish your site as static files, without needing a backend.

Explore the project on GitHub

How it works

The application handles the structure and navigation, while the content lives in separate HTML fragments organized by language. This makes it possible to update texts and other information without changing the Vue components, and to keep versions of the content for different audiences.

Customize your site

Themes

Colors, fonts, spacing, and other visual details can be adjusted to reflect your project's identity. Theme settings help maintain a consistent presentation across the different regions of the site.

Learn more about themes

Templates

Templates organize the layout, defining regions such as the header, main content, footer, and sidebar. Today, Taberna has default.vue as its only available template. New templates are planned to offer other ways of organizing the site.

Learn more about templates

Components and utilities

Components such as the carousel and the language switcher offer reusable features. To handle presentation, the project combines Tailwind CSS with its own utilities that help apply colors, spacing, borders, and other visual elements.

Start your project

Fork Taberna on your GitHub account to keep your own copy. From there, customize the texts, the brand, the images, and the visuals, tracking your changes in the repository.

When you are ready, the project generates the files you can publish on static hosting. That way, you stay in control of the content, the customizations, and the choice of where to host your site.
