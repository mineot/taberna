About Taberna

A ready-made foundation for different stories, projects, and identities.

Before it is a collection of pages and components, Taberna is a starting place. It provides an organized foundation for anyone who wants to build an independent presence on the internet without turning every new idea into a project that must begin from scratch.

Its purpose is not to decide what a website should be, but to take care of the structure people need so they can focus on their content, identity, and the connections they want to create.

Why this name?

A tavern is a place for passing through and staying awhile. People arrive from different places, sit around a table, and share stories, news, work, and plans. The space offers shelter, but the people are what give it meaning.

On the internet, Taberna begins with the same image. The application prepares the place: navigation, pages, visual identity, and presentation tools. What turns this structure into a unique website is what you decide to publish inside it.

The structure prepares the gathering. The content is what makes someone want to stay.

A foundation, not a template

Ready-made templates help people get started, but they can make different projects look the same. Taberna seeks a different balance: it provides technical decisions and reusable components without determining the message, appearance, or purpose of the result.

The same foundation can introduce a person, collect a portfolio, follow a creative project, explain a service, publish articles, or organize information for a small initiative. The content can change completely while the structure remains understandable and easy to maintain.

Content first

The message at the center

Text, pages, and images remain separate from the application's logic. This makes it easier to find, review, and replace what will be presented.

Start simple

Room to evolve

A project can begin with only a few pages and gain new languages, content, and components as its needs become clearer.

Distinct identity

A recognizable space

The name, logo, colors, images, and language can reflect the project's personality instead of reproducing the appearance of a generic platform.

Technology in service of content

Vue, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS form Taberna's technical foundation. These tools support a responsive experience, reusable components, and a development process prepared for change.

Pinia manages language, configuration, and loading state; Vue Router provides hash-based navigation; Lucide supplies the icons; DOMPurify protects loaded HTML; and Vitest verifies the project's automated behavior.

For publishers, however, the most important part remains close to the content. JSON files define identity and navigation; HTML files hold the pages; and web components organize presentation without requiring every section to be built directly in the application's code.

Open to adaptation

Taberna's source code is available under the Apache 2.0 license. This allows you to study, use, modify, and distribute the project according to the terms of the license.

The structure is intended to serve as a starting point, not a boundary. You can replace the content, transform the theme, create new components, or develop paths that are not yet part of the current version.

Who maintains the project

Taberna is a project by Mineot. Its development aims to maintain a clear, flexible foundation that can support personal projects and small initiatives without hiding the content behind an unnecessarily complex structure.

This page will continue to evolve alongside the project. New features may be introduced, examples may change, and the documentation may grow, but the central idea remains: to provide a space that other people can turn into something of their own.
